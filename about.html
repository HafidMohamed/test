From a Garage to Germany's Most Loved Roastery

It started in 1998 when founder Heinrich Bauer converted his garage into a tiny roastery with a 5-kg drum roaster he'd bought second-hand from a retiring Swiss roastmaster.

Twenty-six years later, Kaffeerösterei Kreuzau roasts over 8 tonnes of coffee per month, employs 14 people, and ships to customers across 22 countries. But the philosophy hasn't changed: source ethically, roast carefully, ship fresh.

We cup every batch before it leaves the roastery. If it doesn't meet our standard, it doesn't ship. Simple as that.

"Coffee is an agricultural product. The terroir, the harvest, the processing — they all matter. Our job is to bring that story to the cup without getting in the way."

— Heinrich Bauer, Founder & Head Roaster

Our Timeline

1998

The garage roastery

Heinrich Bauer buys a 5-kg drum roaster and starts roasting for friends and the local weekly market in Kreuzau.

2003

First commercial premises

After five years of market sales, we move into our first commercial space on Rurstraße with a 25-kg Probat roaster.

2009

First origin trip

Heinrich visits coffee farms in Colombia for the first time, establishing direct-trade relationships that still exist today.

2015

Online shop launch

We launch our online shop and start shipping across Germany. Within 12 months we're shipping to 8 European countries.

2021

Expansion & new 60-kg roaster

We install a 60-kg Loring Smart Roast roaster, doubling our capacity while reducing energy consumption by 30%.

2024

Multilingual storefront

We launch translated storefronts for Italian, Spanish, and French visitors via our new translation proxy — the page you're reading right now is part of that rollout.
